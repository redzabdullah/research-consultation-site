Research consultations for postgraduate researchers

Stuck on data, methods or the literature? You don’t have to figure it out alone.

I’m Redzuan Abdullah, Senior Librarian for Research & Data at SMU Libraries. I work alongside postgraduate researchers from the first “does this data even exist?” to the final submission. Below are eight real research journeys I’ve supported, shared anonymously, plus two from my own practice, so you can see what we could do together.

      When to reach out

      The doctoral research horizon

      Your programme milestones tell me what you’re likely to need next. The ideal moment to reach out is just before each one, especially from late Year 1 to mid Year 2, when your questions become more specific. But this is a typical path, not a timetable: you’re welcome to get in touch at any stage. Many of the researchers featured here kept coming back throughout their dissertation, from first idea to final submission.
